# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Scope is an open-source project for people with different backgrounds, experience levels and perspectives. Participation should remain respectful, constructive and focused on improving the project.

Examples of expected behavior include:

- discussing technical decisions without attacking the people involved;
- giving and receiving specific, actionable feedback;
- respecting privacy and removing sensitive infrastructure data from public reports;
- acknowledging mistakes and helping resolve misunderstandings;
- keeping issues and pull requests relevant to the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, publication of private information, or sustained hostile communication.

## Scope

This standard applies to the Scope repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and other official project spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `info@vt-global.de`. Reports will be reviewed in confidence. Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact and prior behavior. Good-faith disagreement about technical or project direction is not itself a violation.
